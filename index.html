      FAQ

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Practical answers regarding capital flows, Master Profiles, and carbon verification.

      What does ECF do?
      ECF is an environmental value platform. We help industrial businesses cut emissions, fulfill compliance (BRSR, CBAM, GHG Protocol), and monetize eligible clean tech without carrying permanent ESG salaries or expensive agency retainers.
      How can emission reduction create financial value?
      By avoiding expensive utility and fossil fuel burn, preventing border export penalties, and bundling qualified reductions into international carbon credit off-take contracts.
      What information do I need to provide?
      Only standard industrial data: utility invoices, fuel logs, and physical facility equipment boundaries. You enter this once in your Master Profile.
      Do I have to enter the same information again?
      No. ECF operates on an "Ask Once, Store Once, Reuse Everywhere" model. Your Master Profile carries over across every reporting and monetization module.
      What is Standard vs. Tailor-made vs. Practitioner?
      Standard: Prescribed template, fixed deliverables, online fixed price.
      Tailor-Made: Custom multi-plant corporate analysis and roadmap.
      Practitioner: Diagnostic consultation to prescribe your route.
      What is the Atmanirbhar Score?
      A verified percentage metric scoring your facility's independence from external fossil grids, municipal freshwater networks, and landfill dependencies.
      Can environmental actions qualify for carbon credits?
      Yes. Sub-15 MW rooftop solar, bio-fuel swaps, and heat recovery can be aggregated into pooled international registries (PoA) at zero upfront cost, returning 75% net carbon credit sales directly to your facility.
      Is carbon revenue guaranteed?
      No. All carbon earnings are potential and subject to formal registry eligibility, verified monitoring periods, and institutional buyer market clearance.
