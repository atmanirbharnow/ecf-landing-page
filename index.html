FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about timelines, data requirements, and execution.

What does ECF provide?
ECF provides fractional environmental architecture: senior GHG practitioner leadership, automated audit vaults, and carbon monetization pathways without the overhead of full-time ESG payroll or expensive agency retainers.
Do I need to know my carbon data before starting?
No. You only need your raw utility bills and fuel purchase logs. ECF’s engine and practitioners calibrate your boundary and calculate emissions accurately according to GHG Protocol standards.
What is the difference between Standard, Tailor-Made, and Practitioner?
Standard: Fixed-price, prescribed template for immediate statutory compliance.
Tailor-Made: Custom multi-plant analysis designed for stakeholder communication and customer presentations.
Practitioner: Diagnostic session to establish boundaries when you are unsure of your requirements.
What information do I need to provide?
For standard corporate footprints, 12 months of electricity invoices, fuel consumption logs (diesel, natural gas, biomass), and plant capacity metrics are all that is required to begin.
How long does it take?
Standard corporate carbon footprints and Atmanirbhar scoring are delivered within 5 to 7 business days following data intake submission.
Can ECF help with BRSR / ESG / CBAM?
Yes. We maintain dedicated modules for SEBI BRSR Core indicators, global ESG supply chain questionnaires, and European CBAM export declarations.
Can I start with only one requirement and add others later?
Yes. The system is modular. Most manufacturers begin with Service 01 (GHG/Carbon) or Service 06 (Atmanirbhar), subsequently unlocking decarbonization audits or monetization pools as needed.
What is the Atmanirbhar Score?
It is a metric evaluating your facility's level of self-reliance from external fossil power, municipal freshwater networks, and municipal landfills. It generates a verified certificate for public and buyer recognition.
Can environmental actions create carbon value?
Yes. Sub-15 MW rooftop solar, bio-fuel swaps, and heat recovery installations can be aggregated into pooled international carbon programmes (PoA) at zero upfront cost, unlocking 75% net carbon credit income.
