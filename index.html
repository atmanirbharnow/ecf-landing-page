FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on fractional sustainability, registry aggregation, and onboarding.

What is a Fractional Sustainability Service?
Instead of hiring an expensive full-time ESG manager (₹6L–₹9L/year), you get access to senior certified practitioners and data vaults on-demand at a flat fractional cost.
How does ₹0 Upfront Carbon Monetization work?
Sub-15 MW clean tech assets normally cannot afford individual validation costs. ECF bundles installations into an existing Program of Activities (PoA), covering all audits and returning 75% net revenue to your facility.
Are reports accepted for EU CBAM and SEBI BRSR?
Yes. Methodologies adhere strictly to GHG Protocol standards and MoEF&CC guidelines, accepted across Tier-1 OEM audits and European customs portals.
