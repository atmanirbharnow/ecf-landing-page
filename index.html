FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does ECF do?
ECF is an environmental value architecture platform. We help industrial manufacturers reduce baseline energy burn, comply with domestic and export mandates (BRSR, CBAM), and monetize clean energy investments without the permanent overhead of full-time ESG salaries.
How can emission reduction create financial value?
Through direct utility expense reduction, avoiding border export tariffs, and aggregating verified sub-15 MW clean assets into pooled international carbon credit sales.
Do I have to enter the same information again?
No. You enter common facility data once into your ECF Master Profile. Every reporting, verification, and monetization service pulls directly from this vault.
Is carbon revenue guaranteed?
No. All carbon credit figures are potential projections subject to registry eligibility, accredited verification periods, and prevailing voluntary market clearance prices.
